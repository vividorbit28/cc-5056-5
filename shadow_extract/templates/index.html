Model

In linear light: B = β·E₀ (empty plate) · I_ground = t·B + A · q = A/B (product bounce per unit plate radiance). The shadow is multiplicative attenuation t plus additive light q·B, not a black transparent overlay. Ratios are clipped to a reliable band (B above the noise floor) so I/B cannot blow up as B→0, and t is bounded to [t_min, 1]. Transfer to a new plate re-uses the same t and q: I₂ = B₂·(t + q). Because q is divided by the original plate B, it carries the product's bounce colour — not the original plate's texture.
